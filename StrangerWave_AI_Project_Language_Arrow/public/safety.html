Online Chat Safety Tips

Your safety matters. Follow these tips whenever you chat with someone new.

1. Protect Your Personal Information

Never share your password, bank details, OTP, home address, live location, or identity documents with strangers.

2. Think Before Sharing Photos

Only share content you are comfortable sharing. Remember that another person may save or screenshot messages and images.

3. Be Careful With Links and Money Requests

Avoid suspicious links, downloads, investment offers, and requests to send money. Never share banking OTPs.

4. Stop Uncomfortable Conversations

If someone threatens you, harasses you, or makes you uncomfortable, stop the conversation and disconnect.

5. Be Respectful

Respect other users. Do not threaten, bully, harass, or share another person's private information.

6. If You Are in Danger

Save relevant evidence when safe to do so, tell someone you trust, and contact the appropriate local authorities if necessary.

Remember: You do not have to continue a conversation that feels unsafe.
