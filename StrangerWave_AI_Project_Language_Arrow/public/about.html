What is StrangerWave?

StrangerWave is an online chat platform designed to help people connect with new people and have conversations online.

Our goal is to make starting a conversation simple and accessible. You can choose your chat profile and look for someone to talk to.

Our Approach to Safety

Please do not share passwords, financial details, your home address, or other sensitive personal information with strangers. Stop chatting if someone makes you uncomfortable.

Please read our safety tips and Terms & Conditions before using the platform.
